How it works

Your files never leave your device.

Most free tools make you upload a file so their server can do the work. Toolspark flips that: the tool comes to your file, and runs inside the browser tab you already have open.

  1. Step 1

    Drop it in or choose it. Your browser reads it straight from your disk into the page.

  2. Step 2
